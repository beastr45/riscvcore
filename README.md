"Load word," she whispered, her voice a melodic 32-bit harmony that bypassed my cache entirely, writing directly to my heart.

"You're so... extensible," I gasped, watching her immediate values encode themselves into my control unit.

When I finally entered her privileged mode, escalating from User to Supervisor to the raw, unprotected Machine level, she didn't trap.
